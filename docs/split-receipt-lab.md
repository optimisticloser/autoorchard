# Split Receipt Lab

The flagship use case for `autoorchard` is a bounded Apple-native receipt pipeline lab.

## Fixed surfaces

- receipt fixtures / corpus
- expected outputs
- score contract
- protected baselines
- replay harness

## Mutable surfaces

- OCR normalization strategy
- line-grouping strategy
- placeholder suppression rules
- wrapped-line merge heuristics
- candidate scoring and reconciliation policy
- abstention / review policy thresholds
- Foundation Models prompt policy (if used)

## Example score dimensions

- item fidelity
- subtotal fidelity
- total fidelity
- service charge fidelity
- truthful abstention
- correction burden proxy

## Why this is a strong flagship

- concrete
- product-facing
- measurable
- Apple-native
- relevant to many app developers working with local AI, OCR, and structured extraction
