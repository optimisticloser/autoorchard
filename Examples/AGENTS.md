# AGENTS.md — Examples

Examples are not filler. They are the proof that autoorchard is a real framework.

## Current examples

- `SplitReceiptLab`
- `MusicGenieRankLab`

## Rules

- keep examples narrow and believable
- do not widen claims beyond what the harness can actually score
- use examples to demonstrate fixed scenarios, bounded mutable surfaces, score contracts, and ledger-backed iteration
- Split is the flagship example and should stay the clearest and strongest one

## Priority

If an example becomes vague, refocus it until a reader can immediately see:
- the product problem
- the mutable surface
- the score
- why autonomous experimentation helps
